# Humanoid Locomotion Diagram

## Diagram Title: Humanoid Robot Locomotion - Walking Patterns and Balance Control

### Description:
This diagram illustrates the complex locomotion patterns used by humanoid robots, including bipedal walking, balance control mechanisms, and gait generation strategies.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Bipedal Walking Phases**:
   - Double support phase
   - Single support phase
   - Swing phase
   - Ground contact patterns

2. **Balance Control**:
   - Zero Moment Point (ZMP) control
   - Center of Mass (CoM) management
   - Capture Point concept
   - Feedback control systems

3. **Gait Generation**:
   - Walking pattern generation
   - Footstep planning
   - Dynamic balance maintenance
   - Obstacle avoidance

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate the complex locomotion systems required for humanoid robots to walk stably in human environments.

# Human-Robot Interaction Diagram

## Diagram Title: Human-Robot Interaction in Physical AI Systems

### Description:
This diagram shows the various modalities and considerations for effective human-robot interaction in physical AI systems, including communication channels, safety protocols, and collaborative behaviors.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Communication Modalities**:
   - Visual communication (gestures, facial expressions)
   - Auditory communication (speech, sounds)
   - Haptic feedback
   - Proxemic behavior

2. **Safety Considerations**:
   - Collision avoidance
   - Force limitation
   - Emergency stop protocols
   - Predictable behavior patterns

3. **Collaborative Behaviors**:
   - Shared workspace management
   - Intention recognition
   - Task coordination
   - Trust building mechanisms

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate the complex interaction systems required for humanoid robots to work safely and effectively alongside humans.

# AI Reasoning Diagram

## Diagram Title: AI Reasoning in Robotics - Planning, Learning, and Decision Making

### Description:
This diagram illustrates the different types of AI reasoning used in robotics, including planning systems, learning algorithms, and decision-making processes that enable robots to operate intelligently in dynamic environments.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Planning Systems**:
   - Path planning algorithms
   - Task planning
   - Motion planning
   - Temporal planning

2. **Learning Systems**:
   - Reinforcement learning
   - Imitation learning
   - Transfer learning
   - Online adaptation

3. **Decision Making**:
   - State estimation
   - Uncertainty handling
   - Multi-objective optimization
   - Real-time decision making

4. **Integration with Physical Systems**:
   - Perception-action coupling
   - Real-world constraints
   - Safety considerations

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate how AI reasoning systems enable robots to plan, learn, and make decisions in the context of physical interaction with the real world.

- Alt-text: "Diagram showing AI reasoning components in robotics including planning systems, learning algorithms, and decision-making processes integrated with physical systems"

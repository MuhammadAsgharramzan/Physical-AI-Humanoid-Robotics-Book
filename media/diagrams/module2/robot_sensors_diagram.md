# Robot Sensors Diagram

## Diagram Title: Types of Robot Sensors - Proprioceptive vs Exteroceptive

### Description:
This diagram categorizes and illustrates the different types of sensors used in robotics, distinguishing between proprioceptive sensors (monitoring internal state) and exteroceptive sensors (perceiving external environment).

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Proprioceptive Sensors**:
   - Joint encoders (position, velocity)
   - Inertial Measurement Units (IMU) - orientation, acceleration
   - Force/torque sensors
   - Temperature sensors

2. **Exteroceptive Sensors**:
   - Cameras (monocular, stereo, RGB-D)
   - LIDAR (distance measurements)
   - Sonar/ultrasound
   - Tactile sensors
   - Range sensors

3. **Robot Body Integration**:
   - Sensor placement on robot body
   - Data flow to processing unit

### Purpose:
To visually categorize and explain the different types of sensors used in robotics, helping readers understand how robots perceive both their internal state and external environment.

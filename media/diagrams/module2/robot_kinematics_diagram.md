# Robot Kinematics Diagram

## Diagram Title: Robot Kinematics - Forward and Inverse Kinematics

### Description:
This diagram illustrates the fundamental concepts of robot kinematics, showing how forward kinematics calculates end-effector position from joint angles, and how inverse kinematics determines joint angles needed to achieve a desired end-effector position.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Forward Kinematics**:
   - Input: Joint angles (θ1, θ2, θ3...)
   - Process: Mathematical transformation
   - Output: End-effector position (x, y, z)

2. **Inverse Kinematics**:
   - Input: Desired end-effector position (x, y, z)
   - Process: Mathematical solution
   - Output: Required joint angles (θ1, θ2, θ3...)

3. **Robot Arm Visualization**:
   - Multiple joints and links
   - Coordinate systems
   - Workspace visualization

4. **Locomotion Kinematics**:
   - Leg kinematics for walking robots
   - Gait patterns
   - Balance and stability considerations

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate the mathematical relationships that govern how robots move their limbs and navigate through space, fundamental to humanoid robotics.

# Physical AI Conceptual Diagram

## Diagram Title: Physical AI vs Traditional AI Comparison

### Description:
This diagram illustrates the fundamental difference between traditional AI and Physical AI (Embodied AI). It shows how traditional AI operates in digital spaces while Physical AI interacts directly with the physical world through sensors and actuators.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Traditional AI Block**:
   - Input: Data/Symbols
   - Processing: Computational Intelligence
   - Output: Data/Symbols
   - Environment: Digital/Virtual Space

2. **Physical AI Block**:
   - Input: Physical Sensors (Vision, Touch, Sound, etc.)
   - Processing: Computational Intelligence + Embodied Cognition
   - Output: Physical Actions (Movement, Manipulation)
   - Environment: Real Physical World
   - Feedback Loop: Continuous sensorimotor loop

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate how Physical AI differs from traditional AI by emphasizing the importance of physical interaction with the real world as a fundamental component of intelligence.

- Format: SVG (for scalability and accessibility)
- Color Palette: Colorblind-friendly (avoid red-green combinations)

- Dimensions: 800x600 pixels

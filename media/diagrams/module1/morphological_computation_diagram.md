# Morphological Computation Diagram

## Diagram Title: Morphological Computation in Physical AI

### Description:
This diagram illustrates how the physical form of a system can contribute to its intelligence. It shows how the body's structure, materials, and form can perform computations that would otherwise require complex algorithms.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Traditional Computation Approach**:
   - High computational load
   - Complex algorithms
   - Detailed modeling required

2. **Morphological Computation Approach**:
   - Physical form contributes to computation
   - Natural dynamics and compliance
   - Simplified control requirements

3. **Example Scenarios**:
   - Passive dynamic walking (energy-efficient due to body structure)
   - Compliant hands that adapt to object shapes
   - Sensor placement that simplifies perception tasks

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate how the physical form of a system can perform computations, reducing the burden on computational systems and enabling more efficient robotic behaviors.

# Symbol Grounding Diagram

## Diagram Title: The Symbol Grounding Problem in AI

### Description:
This diagram illustrates the symbol grounding problem, showing how abstract symbols in traditional AI systems lack connection to real-world experiences, contrasting with embodied approaches that ground symbols in physical interactions.

### Diagram Elements:
1. **Traditional AI - Symbol Grounding Problem**:
   - Abstract symbols (A, B, C)
   - Symbol manipulation rules
   - No connection to real-world experiences
   - Problem: How do symbols connect to reality?

2. **Physical AI - Grounded Symbols**:
   - Symbols connected to sensory experiences
   - Physical interaction with objects
   - Direct connection between symbols and real-world experiences
   - Solution: Symbols grounded in physical interaction

3. **Embodied Grounding**:
   - Sensorimotor experiences
   - Physical object interaction
   - Concept formation through action

### Purpose:
To visually demonstrate the symbol grounding problem in AI and how Physical AI addresses it by grounding symbols in physical interactions with the environment.

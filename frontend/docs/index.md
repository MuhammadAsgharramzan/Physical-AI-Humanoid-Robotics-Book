# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book is designed for students, hobbyists, and early-stage robotics learners who want to understand how artificial intelligence enables machines to interact with the real world through humanoid robots.

## About This Book

This book explains the fundamental principles of Physical AI and humanoid robotics without requiring advanced mathematics. It covers:

- The foundations of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
- Sensing and perception systems in robotics
- Control and actuation in humanoid robotics
- AI reasoning and applications in robotics
- Real-world applications and case studies

## Book Structure

The book is organized into four main modules:

### [Module 1: Foundations of Physical AI](./module1/)
Explore the fundamental concepts of Physical AI, embodied cognition, and how physical interaction with the environment shapes intelligence.

### [Module 2: Sensing & Perception in Robotics](./module2/)
Understand how robots perceive their environment through various sensors and process sensory information to make decisions.

### [Module 3: Control & Actuation in Humanoid Robotics](./module3/)
Learn about the specialized control and actuation systems that enable humanoid robots to move with human-like capabilities.

### [Module 4: AI Reasoning & Applications in Robotics](./module4/)
Discover how artificial intelligence enhances robotic capabilities and enables intelligent behavior in real-world applications.

## Learning Approach

This book takes a practical approach to understanding humanoid robotics, focusing on:

- **Accessibility**: Concepts explained without advanced mathematics
- **Real-world relevance**: Examples and case studies from actual humanoid robots
- **Technical accuracy**: All claims verified against credible sources
- **Progressive learning**: Each module builds on previous concepts

## Target Audience

This book is designed for:
- Students interested in robotics and AI
- Hobbyists exploring humanoid robots
- Early-stage robotics learners
- Tech enthusiasts wanting practical understanding

## Getting Started

Begin with [Module 1: Foundations of Physical AI](./module1/) to establish the core concepts, then progress through each module sequentially for the best learning experience.

## References and Further Reading

Each module includes IEEE-style references for deeper exploration of the topics covered. The book includes over 20 credible sources from robotics textbooks, academic journals, and technical documentation.

# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book is designed for students, hobbyists, and early-stage robotics learners who want to understand how artificial intelligence enables machines to interact with the real world through humanoid robots.

## About This Book

This book explains the fundamental principles of Physical AI and humanoid robotics without requiring advanced mathematics. It covers:

- The foundations of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
- Sensing and perception systems in robotics
- Control and actuation in humanoid robotics
- AI reasoning and applications in robotics
- Real-world applications and case studies

## Learning Approach

This book takes a practical approach to understanding humanoid robotics, focusing on:

- **Accessibility**: Concepts explained without advanced mathematics
- **Real-world relevance**: Examples and case studies from actual humanoid robots
- **Technical accuracy**: All claims verified against credible sources
- **Progressive learning**: Each module builds on previous concepts

## Target Audience

This book is designed for:
- Students interested in robotics and AI
- Hobbyists exploring humanoid robots
- Early-stage robotics learners
- Tech enthusiasts wanting practical understanding

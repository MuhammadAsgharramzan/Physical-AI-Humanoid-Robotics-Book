## Research Summary

### Key Humanoid Systems Components
1. **Locomotion Systems**: Walking, running, stair climbing, balance maintenance
2. **Manipulation Systems**: Multi-fingered hands, arm coordination, grasping
3. **Sensory Systems**: Vision, proprioception, force sensing, balance detection
4. **Control Systems**: Real-time control, whole-body control, task prioritization

### Balance and Locomotion Approaches
1. **Zero-Moment Point (ZMP)**: Classical approach for stable walking
2. **Capture Point/DCM**: Modern approaches for dynamic locomotion
3. **Virtual Model Control**: Human-inspired control strategies
4. **Whole-Body Control**: Multi-task optimization frameworks

### Technical Challenges
1. **Underactuation**: Humanoid robots have fewer actuators than degrees of freedom
2. **Dynamic Balance**: Maintaining balance during motion
3. **Real-time Computation**: Fast control algorithms for dynamic response
4. **Multi-constraint Optimization**: Balancing multiple control objectives

## Application to Physical AI
- Embodiment principles in humanoid design
- Sensorimotor integration for stable locomotion
- Bio-inspired control strategies
- Physical interaction with environment

# Expert Review Process for Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Book

## Overview
This document outlines the expert review process for ensuring technical accuracy and quality of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics book.

## Review Framework

### Robotics Professional Review
**Objective**: Validate technical accuracy of content related to:
- Physical AI and embodied intelligence concepts
- Sensing and perception systems
- Control and actuation in humanoid robotics
- AI reasoning and applications in robotics

**Review Criteria**:
- Technical concepts explained accurately
- Current state-of-the-art information included
- Practical implementation details correct
- Terminology consistent with industry standards
- Mathematical concepts (where present) accurate

### Review Process
1. **Module-based review**: Each of the 4 modules reviewed by domain experts
2. **Technical depth validation**: Ensure appropriate level for target audience
3. **Implementation accuracy**: Verify technical claims against current research
4. **Case study validation**: Confirm real-world examples are accurate

### Quality Assurance Checklist
- [X] All technical claims based on credible sources (IEEE, ACM, arXiv)
- [X] Content reviewed for conceptual accuracy
- [X] Terminology consistent with robotics literature
- [X] Practical examples reflect real-world implementations
- [X] AI and robotics concepts explained accurately

### Reviewer Credentials
Recommended reviewers should have:
- Advanced degrees in robotics, AI, or related fields
- Professional experience in humanoid robotics or Physical AI
- Publications in peer-reviewed robotics conferences (ICRA, IROS, RSS)
- Experience with ROS2, simulation environments, or humanoid platforms

### Review Outcomes
- Technical accuracy verified by domain experts
- Content validated for target audience level
- Implementation details confirmed as accurate
- Industry best practices reflected in content
